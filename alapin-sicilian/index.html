Why does the order of development matter?

In line one, White recaptures on d4 with the queen and Black develops with tempo. In chapter 42, White has already played Nf3. The knight can recapture on d4 instead.

Draw the two attacks on f7.

After 5.Ng5, draw c4-f7 and g5-f7. Play ...d5. The bishop's arrow now hits a pawn, but the knight can still jump to f7. Ask Mishika to name which threat remains.

What changes if White delays c3?

Compare 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 e6 3.c3. Black's e-pawn already stands on e6. In chapter 48 the repertoire uses ...Ne7 and ...d5, with pressure against d4.
